# Community Housing Assistance Program (CHAP) — Eligibility

A household is eligible for CHAP rental assistance when its gross annual income is at or below 50 percent of the area median income, at least one member is a U.S. citizen or qualified non-citizen, and the household rents its primary residence.

Students enrolled full time are eligible only if they live with a parent who is also eligible, or if they have a dependent child.

Eligibility is redetermined every 12 months. A household that misses its redetermination deadline is suspended, not terminated, for 30 days.
