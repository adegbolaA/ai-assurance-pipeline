# CHAP — Income Limits

Income limits are published each April and are based on household size. For program year 2026 the limit is 31,450 dollars for a one-person household and 44,900 dollars for a four-person household.

Countable income includes wages, self-employment earnings, Social Security benefits, unemployment compensation, and child support received. Earned income tax credit refunds are not countable income.

When income falls within 5 percent of the limit, the caseworker must request the two most recent pay statements before making a determination.
